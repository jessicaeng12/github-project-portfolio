Grocery Bag Aid (2023)

Cross-body, adjustable grocery bag aid intended to distribute the load more evenly across the body and accommodate a wide range of users.

Project Overview

As part of a first-year engineering design course, our team was tasked with developing an assistive device. We identified a need for a solution to assist users with upper-limb deficiencies, deformities, or strength discrepancies who may find carrying grocery bags physically and psychologically challenging. The final design featured a cross-body, adjustable grocery bag aid intended to distribute the load more evenly across the body and accommodate a wide range of users. The project followed a complete design process, from problem identification and concept generation to CAD modeling and final presentation.

The grocery bag aid demonstrated a simple, universal solution to an everyday accessibility challenge. The project was awarded first place in its category due to its clean design and broad user demographic. This experience introduced me to the full engineering design process and strengthened my skills in team collaboration, CAD modeling, project organization, and technical communication.

My Role

  • Contributed to concept development, helping refine the problem statement and merge multiple ideas into a feasible solution aligned with team skill sets
  • Designed custom grocery bag hook in Fusion 360, ensuring ergonomic grip and secure attachment to various bag types
  • Took on a project management role, assisting with task delegation, scheduling meetings, and maintaining project organization
  • Created early CAD iterations of the hook using Fusion 360
  • Played a major role in the final presentation, presenting the project at NC State's First-Year Engineering Design Day to a large audience of students, faculty, and external judges

DosEYE Dropper (2026)

Ophthalmic eye-dropper aid for geriatric patients that reduces force needed for administration and improves ease of use.

Project Overview

The DosEYE dropper is a reusable assistive eye-dropper device designed to help geriatric patients administer eye medication. This product assists geriatric patients in the self-administration of ophthalmic medications both safely and independently by improving the accuracy of drop placement and reducing the physical effort required to dispense eye drops, and maintain device integrity.

Our device sucessfully reduced the needed force required to administer opthalmic medication by 88%. Additionally our device did not alter the dosage of a single eye drop, while still being compatible with water soluble preservative free medications. Future improvements include safeties for over exertion of force and guided placement for stable targeting over the eye. Our project was presented at the Senior Design Symposium through the Joint Department of Biomedical Engineering, where it was presented to a multidisciplinary audience of peers and industry professionals.
